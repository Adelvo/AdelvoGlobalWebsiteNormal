FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Still unsure? Apply anyway — we’ll check together if your business is a good fit.

How is this actually free?

For the first 60 days, I don’t charge you any agency or management fee. I do the work for free because I want to prove the service with real results first.

Is there anything I have to pay for?

Yes. You cover the software and infrastructure costs needed to run the campaigns, which are usually around $250/month. We don’t make money from these tools and we’re not affiliated with them.

What exactly do you handle?

I handle the cold email infrastructure, domains, mailboxes, targeting, lead sourcing, copywriting, campaign setup, follow-ups, testing and ongoing optimization.

How long does it take before campaigns start?

Usually around 4 weeks. The inboxes need time to warm up properly. During that time, I build the targeting, lead lists, copy and campaigns so everything is ready to launch.

What happens after the 60 days?

There’s no obligation to continue. If you’re happy with the results and want me to keep running the campaigns, we can discuss continuing on a paid monthly plan.

Who is this for?

This is mainly for B2B businesses with a clear offer and enough client value to make outbound acquisition worthwhile. I’ll check if your business is a good fit before we start.
